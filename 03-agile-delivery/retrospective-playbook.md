# Retrospective Playbook

**Skill:** Continuous improvement · **Competency:** Team facilitation

## Standard format: Data → Insights → Actions (60 min)

| Time | Step | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | **Set the stage** | One-word check-in; restate the Prime Directive |
| 10 min | **Review** | Last retro's actions (done, or dropped?) and the sprint data |
| 15 min | **Gather** | Silent writing into Start / Stop / Continue |
| 10 min | **Group & vote** | Cluster themes; dot-vote (3 votes each) |
| 15 min | **Decide** | Top 1–2 themes become SMART actions |
| 5 min | **Close** | Confidence check, shout-outs |

> **Prime Directive** (Norm Kerth): everyone did the best job they could, given what they knew, their skills, the resources available and the situation at hand.

## Data to bring

- Sprint goal met? (Y / partial / N)
- Planned vs. completed; carry-over and the reasons for it
- Share of unplanned work
- Defects found in-sprint vs. escaped to production

## Action tracker

| Action | Owner | Due | Status | Evidence it worked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lock API contracts before sprint planning | Tech lead | Next planning | Open | Carry-over < 10% |

**Rules**
- At most **2 actions** per retro. A few actions that get done beat a long wish-list.
- An action without an owner doesn't count.

## Rotate formats to avoid fatigue

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| **4Ls:** Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed for | General reflection |
| **Sailboat:** wind, anchors, rocks, island | Goal-oriented teams |
| **Timeline** | Long or eventful sprints |
| **Mad / Sad / Glad** | After a stressful sprint |
