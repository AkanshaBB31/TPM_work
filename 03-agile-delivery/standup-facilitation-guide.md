# Stand-up Facilitation Guide

**Skill:** Agile facilitation · **Competency:** Team flow and blocker removal

## Walk the board, not the people

Go through the board **from right to left**, starting with the items closest to done. Ask *"What will it take to finish this?"* rather than *"What did you do yesterday?"*

## 15-minute structure

1. **Sprint goal check (2 min):** are we on track? 🟢 🟠 🔴
2. **Walk the board (8 min):** focus on items that are blocked, ageing, or in review too long.
3. **Blockers (3 min):** each one gets an owner and an unblock-by time.
4. **Parking lot (2 min):** agree who stays behind for follow-ups.

## Signals to prepare in advance

- Items in the same status for more than 2 days
- Work in progress above the agreed WIP limit
- New high-priority defects since the last stand-up
- Dependencies due this week

## Async variant (distributed teams)

Each person posts **Moved / Next / Blocked**. The facilitator posts a two-line summary, and the team meets only if there are blockers to resolve. See the async update template in [progress reporting](../07-reporting-and-metrics/progress-reporting-templates.md).

## Anti-patterns

- Status reporting to the manager instead of coordinating with the team
- Problem-solving in the stand-up (use the parking lot)
- The same blocker raised three days running with no escalation
