RAID Log Template

Skill: Program governance · Standard: PMBOK risk & issue management

A RAID log keeps Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Dependencies in one place, so nothing a delivery depends on stays implicit. Score risks with the risk management framework.

Definitions

TypeIt is…Key questionTrigger to log
RiskAn uncertain future event that would affect objectivesWhat might happen?"This could…"
AssumptionSomething treated as true without proofWhat are we betting on?"We're planning as if…"
IssueA risk that has already happenedWhat's hurting us now?"This is happening"
DependencyWork owned by someone else that we needWho are we waiting on?"We can't finish until…"

Operating rhythm

WhenWhat
WeeklyOwners update their items; the PM reviews everything scored ≥ 8
FortnightlySteering review of the top 5 risks and any overdue dependencies
MonthlySummary to sponsors: new, closed, trending up
Close-outTurn closed items into lessons learned

Hygiene rules

  • Every row has exactly one named owner and a date.
  • Write risks as "Because of [cause], [event] may occur, leading to [effect]."
  • An assumption left unvalidated for more than 2 weeks becomes a risk. One that turns out false becomes an issue immediately.
  • A dependency without a committed date is a risk by default.
  • Close an issue only when its root cause is addressed. Keep closed rows, with their resolution, so you have history.
