Retrospective Playbook

Skill: Continuous improvement · Competency: Team facilitation

Standard format: Data → Insights → Actions (60 min)

TimeStepActivity
5 minSet the stageOne-word check-in; restate the Prime Directive
10 minReviewLast retro's actions (done, or dropped?) and the sprint data
15 minGatherSilent writing into Start / Stop / Continue
10 minGroup & voteCluster themes; dot-vote (3 votes each)
15 minDecideTop 1–2 themes become SMART actions
5 minCloseConfidence check, shout-outs

Prime Directive (Norm Kerth): everyone did the best job they could, given what they knew, their skills, the resources available and the situation at hand.

Data to bring

  • Sprint goal met? (Y / partial / N)
  • Planned vs. completed; carry-over and the reasons for it
  • Share of unplanned work
  • Defects found in-sprint vs. escaped to production

Board

StartStopContinueShout-outs

Action tracker

ActionOwnerDueStatusEvidence it worked
Lock API contracts before sprint planningTech leadNext planningOpenCarry-over < 10%

Rules

  • At most 2 actions per retro. A few actions that get done beat a long wish-list.
  • An action without an owner doesn't count.
