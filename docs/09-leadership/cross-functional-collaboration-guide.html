Cross-Functional Collaboration Guide

Skill: Leadership & influence · Competency: Aligning engineering, product, QA, support and operations without formal authority

1. Working agreements (set at kickoff)

  • Decision rights: who decides scope, priority, architecture and release (see the RACI)
  • Communication norms: which channel is for what, and the expected response times
  • Shared definitions: the Definition of Ready and Done across functions
  • Conflict path: how disagreements get escalated

2. Facilitation techniques

  • Silent brainstorming first, so the loudest voice doesn't set the direction
  • Disagree and commit: debate openly, then back the decision fully
  • Timebox, with a visible parking lot
  • Close every meeting with its decisions, actions (owner and date) and open questions

3. Influencing without authority

  1. Understand each function's incentives and pressures.
  2. Frame requests in terms of their goals.
  3. Use data to depersonalise disagreements.
  4. Give credit in public and feedback in private.
  5. Make commitments visible, and keep your own.

4. Cross-team dependencies

Cross-team dependencies are the biggest source of friction in collaboration. Manage them explicitly with the dependency board.

5. Health signals
