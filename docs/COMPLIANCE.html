Compliance & Originality Statement

This repository is a personal portfolio of professional knowledge.

It contains

  • Original templates, frameworks and code written by the author
  • Concepts drawn from public industry standards (PMBOK, Scrum Guide, SAFe, ITIL 4, Google SRE, DORA)
  • Fictional examples: the company (Example Co.), teams, systems, people and numbers are all invented

It does not contain

  • Names of any employer, client, product, project, team or internal system
  • Source code, scripts, configuration or infrastructure details from any employer
  • Internal documents, or paraphrases or summaries of them
  • Real metrics, KPIs, SLA/SLO values, ticket data, screenshots or dashboards
  • Customer, employee or financial information
  • Proprietary methodologies or business strategies

Contributor checklist (run before every commit)

  • ☐No company, client, product or codename appears anywhere
  • ☐No real ticket keys. Examples use the fictional EX- prefix only.
  • ☐No internal URLs, hostnames, or cluster, region or environment names
  • ☐Every threshold and metric is illustrative and labelled as such
  • ☐No screenshots or exports from internal tools
  • ☐Content was written fresh, not copied, edited or summarised from internal material
  • ☐The commit author email is a personal address
