The problem

Checkout was a module inside a legacy monolith. It was slow (2.8 s p95), fragile (24% of changes failed) and released only once a month. 18 P1/P2 defects were open.

Scope

In: new checkout UI, checkout service, Kestrel Pay integration, saved carts.
Out: loyalty points, international tax.

Stakeholder move that mattered

The Head of Support was Concerned, because the last checkout change had caused a spike in tickets.

Weeks 21–24 · Release

Ready by criteria, not by confidence

Operational readiness review Change gate & go/no-go Readiness checklist Rollback plan

The ORR found 2 gaps, both closed

  1. There was no alert on payment success rate, only on HTTP errors. Added. (This alert caught the incident later.)
  2. The load test only reached 1.2× peak. Re-run at 2×, it exposed a connection-pool limit, which was fixed.

Go/no-go · 4 June

Go with conditions: phased rollout via flags, the payment-success alert live, and flag rollback rehearsed (4 min).

Week 23 · Incident

Detected in 3 minutes, resolved in 38

At 25% rollout, 3.1% of card payments for one card network failed under the lunchtime peak.

Incident communication Post-incident review

Timeline · 11 June (SEV-2)

  1. 12:41Lunchtime traffic peak
  2. 12:44Payment-success alert fires (added by the ORR)
  3. 12:47SEV-2 declared; I take the comms lead role; first update posted
  4. 12:58Cause narrowed to a 2 s vendor timeout that was too low under peak
  5. 13:13Rollout flag turned off; traffic back on the legacy path
  6. 13:19Resolved: payment success back to baseline

Post-incident actions

TypeAction
PreventValidate timeout values in config review; include vendor latency in load tests
DetectAutomated canary analysis between rollout stages
MitigateTimeout raised to 5 s, with retry and backoff

Rollout resumed on 17 June and reached 100% on 22 June with no further incidents.

Week 26 · Close-out

Lessons learned

What workedWhat I'd change
Contract-first integration recovered about 2 weeks after the vendor sandbox failedPut sandbox SLAs in vendor contracts from the start, not at renewal
The ORR added the alert that detected the incident in 3 minutesMake 2× peak load testing a default criterion, not an ORR finding
Phased rollout plus flag rollback limited impact to 25% of traffic for 38 minutesUse automated canary analysis from the first stage
Early escalation with options got a decision within 48 hoursBaseline success metrics earlier. The latency baseline was a PRD gap
Automated digests and hygiene checks made governance cheaper (3 h → 45 min a week)
