Lightweight governance that keeps a program honest: visible risks, recorded decisions, baselined commitments and well-managed vendors.

02Technical Program Management →

The technical side of program leadership: chartering multi-team programs, managing cross-team technical dependencies, and making sure systems are operable before they launch.

  • Program charter templateAlign sponsors and teams on outcomes, scope, governance and success measures before work starts

03Agile Delivery →

Running predictable sprints: getting work ready, planning to real capacity, facilitating the team, and reporting results honestly.

  • Definition of ready and backlog decompositionReview requirements for readiness and break them into sprint-sized stories
  • Sprint planning playbookCalculate capacity and run sprint planning
  • Standup facilitation guideRun stand-ups that remove blockers instead of reciting status
  • Sprint reporting and forecastingReport sprint outcomes, track predictability and forecast the backlog

04Release Management →

Shipping predictably and recovering safely. Readiness is decided by criteria and evidence, not by confidence.

  • Release readiness checklistCheck scope, quality, operations, business readiness and governance for a release candidate
  • Change readiness gate and go no goEvaluate changes against objective gate criteria and run a 30-minute go/no-go
  • Rollback planning templateDefine rollback triggers, strategy and steps before release day
  • Release notes frameworkCapture change metadata at merge time and compile notes for each audience
05Quality & Reliability →

Defining, tracking and enforcing service levels for defects and incidents, with early warnings instead of after-the-fact breach reports.

  • Defect slo frameworkSet resolution targets by priority, classify defects, report attainment and alert before breaches
  • Defect triage playbookTurn incoming defect reports into prioritised, owned work
  • Post incident review templateRun blameless reviews and track corrective actions
  • Incident communication playbookCommunicate clearly and on a cadence during incidents
06Stakeholder Management →

Operating principles

  1. Criteria over opinion. "Ready" means the stated criteria are met.
  2. Surface risk early. Warn before breaches, and escalate before there are surprises.
  3. Automate the gathering, not the judgment. Scripts collect; people decide.
  4. One source of truth. Reports come from the tracker, never copied by hand.
  5. Trends over snapshots. Direction tells you more than a single value.
